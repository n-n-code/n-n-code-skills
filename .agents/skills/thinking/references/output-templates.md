# Thinking output templates

Read this only when a thinking pass needs more structure than the small-decision output in `SKILL.md`. Omit sections that do not help the decision.

## Medium decision

```markdown
## Decision, goal, and rationale
What should happen, what success means, and the decisive reason.

## Constraints and assumptions
What must remain true, any material uncertainty, and what would change the decision.

## Options
Compare only serious alternatives on the factors that decide the outcome.

## Next move
The smallest credible action or experiment, with an owner or dependency when known.

## Validation
The evidence that confirms success, failure, or the need to reconsider.
```

## Large or systemic decision

```markdown
## Decision, goal, and rationale
The recommendation, why it matters now, and the decisive reason.

## System view
Relevant actors, incentives, dependencies, feedback loops, and second-order effects.

## Evidence, assumptions, and risks
What is known, what is inferred, any uncertainty material to the decision, and what would change the conclusion.

## Options and trade-offs
Compare the strongest approaches on value, cost, risk, reversibility, and time to validate.

## Next moves
Ordered actions or experiments with important dependencies.

## Validation and recovery
Success and failure signals, decision checkpoints, and rollback when an action can cause material harm.
```
